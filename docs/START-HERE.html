CoE5 Spell & Weapon Workbench

0.18.1 · Windows x64 · portable

  1. Extract the whole ZIP into a writable folder.
  2. Open CoE5Workbench.exe. Keep its other folders beside it.
  3. Choose Explore Vanilla spells & weapons, Open your mod or a new definition. Press F1 for help.

.NET is included. If the app asks for WebView2, use Microsoft's Evergreen Runtime, then open Workbench again. Nothing installs automatically.

Try the editor

Open an included file from examples. Find a spell or weapon, edit a supported value, Apply changes, then Export copy. The original stays intact. Export before closing; open drafts are not automatically restored.

See it in game

All integrated tests require your own supported CoE5 5.43 and official CoE5SE 3.3.0, 3.2.2 or 2.13.0 files. Selected-spell, Fireball AI and mixed-comparison cases were observed on 3.3.0; preparation alone is not a successful game test. Editing and native export work offline.

Use Test selected or Observe original for an eligible entry. Choose game/extender files if requested, Prepare, then Start separately. Normal mods, profiles and saves are not used.

Update or remove

Export your work and close the app. Extract updates into a new folder; do not merge over a used copy. Remove the extracted folder to remove the portable app. Saved tests remain on your Windows account. Share the original ZIP, not a used app folder.
