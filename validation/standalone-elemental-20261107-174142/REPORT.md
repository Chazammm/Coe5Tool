## Basis and offline checks

The native damage table defines fire, cold and shock as types 4–6. A natural weapon with `dmg 0` takes its base damage from monster modding. The regular melee/ranged attack declarations accept a numeric payload. For special damage and benefit types those attack-row numbers instead encode masks. The game manual describes elemental resistances and explains the possible cold/numbness interaction. The Workbench’s 0–99 base and 1–99 row limits bound this fixture. They do not predict an individual damage roll. [Illwinter modding manual](https://www.illwinter.com/coe5/coe5modding.html) · [Illwinter game manual](https://www.illwinter.com/coe5/coe5manual.html).

CoE5SE 2.13.0 documents the row type, weapon and payload readers, and separate Windows resistance getters for living units. The field implementation creates its defender on square (1,1) under slot 24. The fixed helper takes a read-only sample for the unique matching Spearman on that square after opening. Comparisons continue to mutate only the three visuals on the privately owned weapon. They do not record pre-battle resistance in their saved visual samples.
