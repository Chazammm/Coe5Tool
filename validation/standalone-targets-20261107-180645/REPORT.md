## Target selection and coverage

Open a supported native weapon, choose its **Test … with CoE5SE…** action, then **Choose target…**. Search by partial name, number or `#ID`. **Use choice** places one opponent in the setup. Spearman #13 is the default and is available for package preparation without an installed catalog. Desktop operation against any selection still requires the verified game and extender.

The reader examined native slots 0–1999 of the fingerprinted local executable. It returned **1,847 candidates**, including 74 groups of duplicate names. It excluded 148 unnamed slots, one nonpositive-HP record and four placeholder/debug records. Each candidate keeps its ID, original name and signed base HP. The picker labels the data **Installed 5.43**. The rebuilt Windows app displayed separate rows for Captain #12 and Captain #291; selecting #291 identified that slot in the test setup. The unit list contains no historical or mod-added rows. Catalog audit (local evidence) · Rendered duplicate-name search (local evidence).

Native preparation checks the chosen ID and exact name in the verified executable. At launch the helper checks the loaded name and positive HP. After opening the field it requires exactly one defender for slot 24 on square (1,1) and verifies its monster ID. A mismatched or ambiguous spawn aborts before combat. The selected element determines the living-unit resistance getter; failure to obtain a numeric reading is recorded as unavailable. The fixed field exposes the reading under **Attack details**. Each comparison opens a fresh field and saves its own reading. The native result reader checks the defender against the prepared candidate. Only selected visual values participate in the existing draft-return workflow. ## Limits

Only these two cold ranged battles were run for this change. Selection and rejection for melee were checked offline. Base HP is record metadata; current HP and elemental resistance can depend on the spawned unit. A searchable record can still have special behavior, and runtime identity checks can refuse a field. No resistance is inferred from a damage roll or from an effect's appearance. The two encounters do not establish a damage formula, general AI behavior, a VFX verdict, or campaign/save compatibility. This fixture supports one native opponent at a time. Mixed groups, waves, mod-loaded target catalogs and Vanilla-only test fields remain outside it.
